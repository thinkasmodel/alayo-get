翻译在本机完成，文字不会发给任何服务。第一次翻译某种语言时，Chrome 会从 Google 下载这个语言的翻译模型（约 80 MB），之后不用联网。Translation runs on this device. Your text isn’t sent to any service. The first time you translate a language, Chrome downloads its translation model from Google (about 80 MB). After that, no connection is needed.
